Partner program terms

Version of 9 November 2026.

1. What it is

You share your personal link to @iSpectrumAccessBot. When someone you referred pays for iSpectrum Private Research, you earn a commission. The program has one level only: you earn nothing on people referred by your referrals.

2. Your link

Open @iSpectrumAccessBot and send /partner. Your link looks like t.me/iSpectrumAccessBot?start=ref_<code>.

3. Commission

  • 30% of the amount the referred user actually pays for their first payment.
  • 20% of each renewal they pay during the 6 months after their first payment.
  • Commission is calculated on the amount paid after any discount, taxes and refunds.

4. Attribution

  • First link wins. A user is credited to the first partner link they open in the bot.
  • The referred user must make the first payment within 30 days of opening your link. If they don't, the attribution expires.
  • Only new customers count, meaning people who have never paid for iSpectrum before.
  • No self-referral. You cannot earn on your own purchases or on accounts you control.

5. Discount for your referrals

New users who arrive through a partner link get 10% off their first month (72 USD instead of 80 USD). For now this applies to in-bot payment with Telegram Stars only.

6. Hold and refunds

Each commission is held for 30 days before it becomes payable. If the payment is refunded, disputed or reversed, the commission is cancelled. If it was already paid out, it may be deducted from future commissions.

7. Payouts

  • Payouts are made in USDT via @CryptoBot to the Telegram account that owns your partner link.
  • The minimum payout is 10 USD. Smaller balances carry over.
  • To receive payouts you must have started @CryptoBot at least once. If a direct transfer is not possible, we may send a check that only your account can claim.
  • You are responsible for any taxes on your commissions.

8. How you may promote

  • No spam. No unsolicited DMs, no mass messages, no posting links in chats or groups where you are not allowed to advertise, and no fake accounts.
  • No promises of profit. Do not claim or imply earnings, profits, win rates, accuracy percentages or “guaranteed” results.
  • No price claims. iSpectrum projects the timing of likely turns. It does not give price levels, price targets or trade signals. Do not describe it as a signal service or as predicting prices.
  • Disclose the relationship. Make it clear that you earn a commission through your link, for example “affiliate link: I earn a commission if you subscribe”. Follow the advertising and disclosure rules where you and your audience live, such as the FTC Endorsement Guides in the US.
  • Do not pretend to be iSpectrum or Telegram, and do not bid on “iSpectrum” brand keywords in paid ads.
  • Not financial advice: say so wherever you present projections.

9. Breaking these rules

We may withhold or cancel commissions earned in breach of these terms and remove you from the program.

10. Changes

iSpectrum may change or end the program by announcing it in the bot or channel with at least 14 days' notice. Commissions already earned under the old terms will still be paid according to those terms.

Questions: send /support in @iSpectrumAccessBot.
