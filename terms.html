After payment you receive a one-time access key. Access is granted when the key is activated in the Telegram bot @iSpectrumAccessBot. A key can be activated once, by one Telegram account. Keep the key private until you activate it; we cannot restore a key that someone else activated.

A licence key adds 30 days. If a licence is active, the days are added to its end date. When the period ends without renewal, the licence and the included channel access end. There is no automatic renewal.

Screenshots are generated on request from the latest published projection and recent MT4 prices. If current data is not available, the bot does not charge you. The balance and prepaid screenshots are not withdrawable and are not transferable to another Telegram account.

Payments are processed by Crypto Pay (cryptocurrency) or Whop (cards and other methods) under their own terms. If a payment was taken but no key was issued, or the key cannot be activated, contact support in the bot and we will issue a key or a refund. Activated digital access is generally not refundable.
